# RetrievalLabs

RetrievalLabs is a non-profit organization dedicated to hosting and developing open-source projects focused on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and AI retrieval systems. We aim to advance the state of AI retrieval technologies through collaborative, community-driven development.

## Mission

We provide tools, frameworks, and resources to help developers build robust AI retrieval solutions that enhance the capabilities of large language models and other AI systems.
